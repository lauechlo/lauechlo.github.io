Right now that means Hoagie products, a lab I started for non-CS students, and this past summer on Disney+ Search.

About me

Music was my first love, and there is always more music in the world to discover. Similarly, I love connecting with different kinds of communities and building / leading things that will leave a positive impact. Outside of work, I play the harp, sing jazz a cappella, hike wherever I can find a trail, and boulder. I also do calligraphy and crochet, write poems, and log every new restaurant and dessert shop I try on Beli.

I love pushing myself out of my comfort zone, and went from singing in the shower to singing with the Princeton Katzenjammers, the oldest co-ed a cappella group in the Ivy League. I'm the president, and I've led the group on tour to Montreal, with DC and Korea coming up. Take a listen to our music :)
