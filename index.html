I started the harp at four because Minnie Mouse played one on Mickey Mouse Clubhouse. Music made me curious about what motivates people and what brings them together, so I came to Princeton for psychology. Product turned out to be the work I like most: learning something new until I'm good at it, then getting people from very different backgrounds to build in the same direction.

Right now that means Hoagie products, a lab I started for non-CS students, and this past summer on Disney+ Search.

About me

Music was the first thing I was ever serious about. What I keep finding is that I love connecting with different kinds of communities, and building things that actually change something for them. Outside of work I play the harp, hike whenever I can find a trail, and log every new restaurant and dessert shop I try on Beli.

Making music with people is also how I ended up in the Princeton Katzenjammers, the oldest co-ed a cappella group in the Ivy League. I'm the president, and I've led the group on tour to Montreal, with DC and Korea coming up. This is what we sound like, recorded for our 50th anniversary.
