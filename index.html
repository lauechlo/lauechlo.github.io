I started playing the harp when I was four because Minnie Mouse played one on a Mickey Mouse Clubhouse episode. Music made me curious about how it's able to unite and bring people from all backgrounds together, so I came to Princeton to study Psychology. Product turned out to be a mix of both: learning something new, then getting people with different backgrounds to build together in the same direction.

Right now that means Hoagie products, a lab I started for non-CS students, and this past summer on Disney+ Search.

About me

Music was my first love, and there is always more music in the world to discover. Similarly, I love connecting with different kinds of communities and building / leading things that will leave a positive impact. Outside of work, I play the harp, sing jazz a cappella, hike wherever I can find a trail, and boulder. I also do calligraphy and crochet, write poems, and log every new restaurant and dessert shop I try on Beli.

I love pushing myself out of my comfort zone, and went from singing in the shower to singing with the Princeton Katzenjammers, the oldest co-ed a cappella group in the Ivy League. I'm the president, and I've led the group on tour to Montreal, with DC and Korea coming up. We traveled to Tokyo in winter 2025. This is what we sound like, recorded for our 50th anniversary.
